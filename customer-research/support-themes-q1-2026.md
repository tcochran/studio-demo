# Music Listener Survey — 500 Respondents, Ages 18-34

## How People Get Music Today

- 61% download illegally (Napster, LimeWire, BitTorrent)
- 28% buy CDs
- 8% buy from iTunes ($0.99/song)
- 3% listen to internet radio (Pandora)

## Biggest Frustrations

1. **"I can't try before I buy"** (72%) — Heard a song on the radio, have to buy the whole album to own it
2. **"Downloads are unreliable"** (58%) — Wrong files, viruses, corrupted tracks, slow downloads
3. **"Music is too expensive"** (54%) — $15/album or $0.99/song adds up fast for heavy listeners
4. **"I can't take it everywhere"** (41%) — iPod has limited storage, can't access your library from a friend's computer
5. **"Discovery is broken"** (38%) — Radio plays the same 20 songs, no way to explore by mood or genre

## Willingness to Pay for Streaming

- 67% would pay $5-10/month for unlimited legal streaming
- 23% would only use it if free (ad-supported)
- 10% would never pay — "music should be free"
