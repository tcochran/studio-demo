# Top 3 Features for Launch

## 1. Instant Playback

Search for any song and play it immediately. No download, no wait. This is the entire pitch — if this doesn't work flawlessly, nothing else matters.

**Must have:**
- Search by song name, artist, or album
- Play starts within 2 seconds on broadband
- Basic controls: play, pause, skip, volume

## 2. Playlists

Let users create and save playlists. This is how people organize music today (mix CDs, iPod playlists) — we need to match that mental model.

**Must have:**
- Create, rename, delete playlists
- Add/remove songs
- Share a playlist via link

## 3. Free Tier with Ads

We can't charge people upfront for something they've never tried. Free tier gets them in the door. Audio ads between songs, upgrade to Premium to remove them.

**Must have:**
- 30-second audio ad every 3-4 songs
- Banner ad on player screen
- Clear upgrade prompt: "Go Premium — no ads, offline mode, $9.99/month"
